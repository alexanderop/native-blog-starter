## Begin with something small

A blog can be a place to think in public. It does not need a grand launch. Write down one thing you learned, explain the part that surprised you, and give it a useful title.

This sample is yours to replace. Change the site identity, delete these posts, and bring your own interests. Your words should be the interesting part.

## Keep the files open

Every article becomes a complete page. The files can live on a simple static host. Readers can follow links, browse the archive, and read the table of contents without JavaScript.

- Write in a local Markdown file.
- Preview the result in your browser.
- Publish the generated directory.

![Three steps connect writing, building, and publishing.](/media/open-files.svg)

## Leave a trail

Link to the things that helped. Add enough context for someone arriving later. A short note with a clear example is often more useful than a long list of conclusions.
